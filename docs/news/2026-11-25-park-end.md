# Parking a running session asks whether to end it too

Press w (or Park) on a session that runs in a terminal of the window, and a dialog asks: End session and park, or Park, keep it running. A parked session that was ended takes no resources, and you resume it from its row; it stays parked.

When the session waits for your next prompt, w then Enter ends and parks it at once. When it is working, asking you something, or skydeck cannot tell, the dialog starts on keeping it running. Space or a letter typed while the dialog asks presses nothing and puts it on Cancel, so a word typed by mistake ends nothing.
