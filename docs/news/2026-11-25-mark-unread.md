# Mark a session unread to come back to it

Looked at a session but want to think about it first? Press `u` in the list, or Unread in the
session's header. It needs you again, with the line `marked unread`, in every window, until you
come back to it: select it again from another row for a moment, or type into it.

Staying on it, reloading the window or a restart keeps the mark. Press `u` again, or Read, to take
it away; on a session that finished or ended unexpectedly, `u` marks it read.
